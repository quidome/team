## Project Brief
## Initial discovery

This is an early discovery capture based on the team's current workflows. It is not a final product definition.

### Current workflow / opportunity

- Duties and transport are currently coordinated in chat.
- The team can see the game program ahead of time, potentially through Christmas, but available Excel sheets may be incomplete.
- The app should support advance planning while allowing informal coordination to remain possible.

### Game program

- Keep relevant games for our team and other teams in one program.
- A game may include date/time, participating teams, location, home/away status, and travel time.
- For non-own games, record our team's required contribution: 0–2 referees and 0–2 jurors.
- Own away games create driving needs; own home games do not.
- Travel time from the usual home venue plus a 30-minute arrival buffer can produce suggested departure times and chat messages. The buffer should be overrideable per game.

### Players and duties

- Player profiles may include name, jersey number, birthday, and association ID.
- Track all duty types, including presence/attendance, refereeing, jury work, driving, extra duties, and future duty types.
- Duties are assigned to players. The actual adult fulfiller is not tracked.
- Away games usually need 2–3 cars. Children may receive driving-duty assignments even though they cannot drive.
- Players can sign up for open duties.
- Assignments can be changed and balanced, but fairness should remain guidance rather than strict enforcement.
- Keep a history of each occurrence: player, duty, related game/event, and its date/time. Record whether the duty was completed; counters and fairness views are derived from this history.

### Import and program sources

- Support manual entry and imports from Excel (`.xls`/`.xlsx`), OpenDocument/ODF spreadsheets (`.ods`), and CSV.
- Imported data may be incomplete, so imports should support preview, column mapping, validation, and manual correction.
- Whether the iOS app has a web interface, export function, or API is an open research question.

### Candidate first slice

1. Maintain players and their details.
2. Enter or import the game program.
3. Generate referee, jury, and driving duties.
4. Announce open duties and collect sign-ups.
5. Adjust assignments and retain the historical log.

### Questions / unknowns

- What app and association provide the program, and can data be exported or accessed through an API?
- Are the 0–2 referee and 0–2 jury requirements entered per game, or determined by competition rules?
- How should the number of driving requirements relate to the 2–3 cars typically needed?
- What additional fields are needed for games, such as opponent, competition, age group, or arrival details?
- Should games be the first event type, with training, meetings, and other team events added later?
- What exactly counts as presence and as an extra duty?

This is an informal discovery document, **not a PRD**. Incomplete, contradictory, tentative and rough thoughts are welcome here. Use it as a comfortable place to dump and develop ideas before they become more structured.
