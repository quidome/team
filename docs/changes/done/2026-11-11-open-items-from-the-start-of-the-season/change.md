every past event of the season that is not checked is an open item. A new item "duty games not
checked"; "games not checked" counts from the start of the season, not from 4 November 2026; an
item of more games opens the list of its games. The behaviour is in the epic's `functional.md`,
story 3; the architecture in its `technical.md`.
