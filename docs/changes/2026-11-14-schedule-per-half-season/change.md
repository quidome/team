# Schedule not entered for each half season

The open item "schedule not entered" counts the home games of a half
season only when enough club teams have their competition for that half, so a home game of one
early team (MSE 1 in January) gives no reminder; then every home game of that half counts, not
only the next 27 days. A club team counts for a half season when one of its competitions has its first game in that half; with home games of the second half the item says how many club teams are known ("second half: 3 of 4 teams known"). The page "Season" does not change, and the list for each club team is parked.
