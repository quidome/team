# The start page

What it does **now**, for the coordinator. Updated at each /close that changes it.

## Purpose
A look of a few seconds tells the coordinator whether something still needs them, and what comes
next. Everything else of the season is one tap further, on [Season](season.md).

## Behaviour
- **No club chosen:** only the choose step ([choose club and team](choose-club-and-team.md)) and the links.
- **A new season without a team:** today's date, the choose step, then the open items and the list of events (with the trainings only), then the links.
- **A team chosen**, from the top: the header; the open items; the list of events (the last duty games, then the coming events); the message links; the links "Season · Attendance · Duties · Report · More" ([report](report.md)). "More" (`/more`) has Team, Players and Settings, with "Back" to the start page. The choice of club and team is on Settings ("Change club or team").
- **The header:** the team ("Blue Drakes M16 1") and today's date ("Tuesday 10 November"). In July and August, when the open items also cover the season before, the season follows the team ("Blue Drakes M16 1 · 2026-2027").
- **The open items**: what still needs the coordinator, one row for each kind, with an orange dot, in this order; a row with a "›" goes to the place to act, a row of more games (with "▾") opens the list of those games below it:
  1. "The last reading of the game list was held back." (a held-back reading waits) and "The games may be out of date since Thu 22 Oct." (3 nights without a good reading): to the "Game list" card on "Season" ([read the game list](read-the-game-list.md)).
  2. "4 changes not seen" ("1 change not seen"): to the [list of changes](list-of-changes.md).
  3. The warnings of the coming games of the team ([attendance at games](game-attendance.md)), each kind one row; for one game a tap opens it, for more a tap opens the list of those games below the item:
     - "Game Sat 31 Oct: 5 of 7 players, 3 no answer" (without "no answer": "Game Sat 31 Oct: 5 of 7 players"), "2 games: too few players": fewer than 7 coming, from 3 days before the date until the start time.
     - "Away game Sat 31 Oct: driver Anna is not coming", "Away game Sat 31 Oct: 2 drivers are not coming", "2 games: a driver is not coming": at once, until the start time.
     - "Game Wed 11 Nov moved: check the answers", "2 games moved: check the answers": answers from before a move, from 3 days before the new date until its start time.
     An item goes away by itself when its problem is gone. A game that left the game list gives no warning. A game marked "Not played" gives no warning.
  4. "Home game Sat 31 Oct: schedule not entered" for one, "5 home games: schedule not entered" for more: the home games of the club from today until 27 days later whose committee schedule is not entered ([duties](duties.md)); gone games, games that are no longer home games of the club, and past games are not in it. For one game a tap opens it; for more, a tap opens the list of those games below the item ("Sat 31 Oct · 14:30 Blue Drakes X14 1 – BV Alphia X14 1"), each a tap to its page, so the item and its list always hold the same games.
  5. "Away game Sat 7 Nov: 1 driver open", "Home game Sat 31 Oct: 1 table place open" (or "1 referee place, 2 table places open") for one, "3 games with open places" for more: the coming games with places of the team that have no player and that count ([duties](duties.md)): table and referee places from today until 27 days later, driver places from today until the Sunday of next week. A driver proposal that rises when a player joins opens a place without a save. For one game a tap opens it; for more, a tap opens the list of those games below the item, as for the schedule.
  6. "Training day Thu 22 Oct not opened" for one, "3 training days not opened" for more: to the oldest of them, which shows "Not opened" with "Open training".
  7. "Training Thu 22 Oct not checked" for one, "2 trainings not checked" for more: to the oldest of them ([trainings and attendance](trainings-and-attendance.md), "Checked").
  8. "Game Sat 31 Oct not checked" for one, "2 games not checked" for more: to the oldest of them. The games of the team before today that are not checked, not "not played", still in the game list, and on or after 4 November 2026 (release 1, from which answers at games were entered) ([attendance at games](game-attendance.md), "After the game").
  Without open items: one grey row "Nothing needs you."
- **Which dates count:** only dates before today (Dutch date); today's training and game are in the list of events. A cancelled training or training day is never an open item. The open items cover the current season and, in July and August, also the season before, so the record of June stays in view after 1 July. In those two months the number of training days not opened can therefore be larger than the line on "Attendance", which counts one season.
- **Past events** show only as open items, with one exception: **the last duty games**. These are the games of the newest date before today that have places of the team (a place that names the team, or drivers) and are not games of the team: home games of other club teams. They are in the current season, or in July and August, when it has none yet, the season before. They stand at the top of the list, under their date, always lighter (the date has passed), so a correction after the game ("Someone else", "Nobody") stays one tap away. A newer date with such games replaces them. A game of the team is not among them: it is an open item until it is checked, and after that it is on [Season](season.md).
- **The coming events**: the games of the team, the home games of other club teams with places of the team, and the training days and trainings from today until the Sunday of next week (on a Sunday: today and the next week). Other possible duty games are not in it. Cancelled trainings show, as "Cancelled" (the switch "Show cancelled" is a setting of "Season" only). A cancelled training does not count as something in the two weeks: when they have nothing else, the list also shows the first coming event of the season after them that is not cancelled; when there is none, "No coming events."
- **The rows of the list:** no cards; grouped by date (a small grey heading, "Tue 10 Nov"), in time order, the start time on the left ("time not known yet" when not known), each row at least 44 pixels high.
  - **A game of the team**: a soft orange background and an orange bar. "Home game against BC Utrecht Cangeroes M16 2" or "Away game at De Dunckers M16 3" (a home game: in the club's own hall, also as the away side against another club team); then the hall and town, with "· Not played" or "· Checked"; the count of who comes ("6 coming · 2 no answer"; from the start time "8 there · 1 absent"; not on a game marked "Not played") ([attendance at games](game-attendance.md)); the places of the team, as on "Season".
  - **Another game with places of the team**: a thin blue bar, no background; the two teams ("Blue Drakes X14 1 – BS Leiden X14 4") and the places of the team ("all filled", "Table 1, 2: Anna, open", "· Schedule not entered"); no hall and town (always the club's own hall).
  - **A training**: no bar; "Training" ("· extra" for an extra training) and its status ("Not opened", "2 absent · Checked", "Cancelled").
  - The warnings of a game show on its row; a game that left the game list is crossed out, with "no longer in the game list".
  - **Lighter** (grey text, lighter bar): an event with nothing left to do: an event before today (the last duty games); a training that is checked or cancelled; a game marked "Not played"; a game of the team that is checked; another game whose places of the team are all filled and whose schedule is entered. All other events have normal text, also another game that left the game list.
- **The message links**: below the list, "Message of the week ›" when this week has a game of the team today or later, or table or referee places of the team, and "Committee message ›" when this week (Monday to Sunday) has table or referee places of the team ([messages](messages.md), decision 0034). Without a team they are not shown.
- A tap on a training or a game in the list, or on a training item or a game of an item, opens it (with `?from=start`); "Back" there returns to the start page.

## Errors and limits
- The start page cannot be loaded: "The start page could not be loaded. Load the page again."
- The coming events cover only the current season: in the last days of June its two weeks can reach into the new season, whose events do not show (an idea in `docs/ideas.md`).

## Interface
- Screens: the start page (`/`); the page "More" (`/more`).
- Server address (needs a login): `GET /api/start`: `today` (the Dutch date), `show_season` (true in July and August), the open items (each with its kind; dates and counts where they apply), `next` (the coming events) and `last_games` (the last duty games, with their season `last_games_season`). Each event is a `StartEvent`: `event` (a game or a training row, as on `GET /api/season`), `look` (`normal` or `lighter`), and for a game of the team `side` (`home` or `away`) and `opponent`. `messages` has `week` and `committee`: whether each link is offered. The item "schedule not entered" has its games (season, id, date, start time, home, away); the item "open_places" has its games with the open places of each kind (`referee`, `table`, `drive`); `too_few_players` has its games with `coming`, `no_answer` and `enough`; `driver_not_coming` its games with the names of the drivers (only in the answer, never in a log); `answers_moved` its games; `games_not_checked` its oldest game (season, id, date) and the count.
- The rules are `team_core::open_items` (the warnings from `game_warnings`, the rule of the page of a game; the games not checked from `games_not_checked`, from the release day in `game_check_start`), `schedule_not_entered`, `open_places_games`, `last_games`, `next_events`, `open_item_seasons`, `event_look` (lighter or normal), `team_side` (home or away, and the opponent; the same rule as the message of the week) and `week_has_duty_places` (the committee link); each open item comes from the rule of its source page (the "Game list" card, the list of changes, the page of a game, "Attendance").
