# Language

The words that all documents, code and conversations about this product use. Only the Vision
stage adds, changes or removes words. Define meaning, not mechanics: no flags, paths or formats.

| Term | Meaning | Use / do not use |
|------|---------|------------------|
| coordinator | The one person who uses the tool and changes its data. Runs one team in each season. | Not "admin", not "user" when the coordinator is meant. |
| team | The youth team the coordinator runs in one season: the team chosen from the game list for that season. Each season has its own team, often with the same players. | Say "other club team" for the club's other teams. In a count or a list of several teams on a screen, "teams" may mean club teams, for example "second half: 3 of 4 teams known". |
| club | The sports club the team belongs to. It has the players and the teams of each season. | |
| season | The year of the club's competitions: from 1 July until 30 June, as the federation counts it. | "2026-2027". |
| half season | The first half (1 July until 31 December) or the second half (1 January until 30 June) of a season: the period of one schedule of the referee committee, for the home games of all club teams. | "The second half". Not for a competition: a senior team has no halves. |
| competition | What a club team plays in the game list. A youth team has one competition for each half season, often in another division in the second half; a senior team has one competition for the whole season. | |
| player | A person of the club that the coordinator keeps in the tool. Has a first name, and usually a last name. Is in the team of a season through a role, or is not in the team. | Not "member", not "child" in the product. |
| role | What a player is in the team of a season: plays games, or only trains. Has a start date, and an end date when the player leaves or changes role. A player's roles, one after the other, are the player's history. | "Plays games" / "Only trains". Not "position", not "membership". |
| hidden | A player or a reason that the tool no longer offers as a choice. Its history and totals stay. | Not "deleted", not "archived". |
| game | One match on the schedule, with a date, start time, place, home team and away team. | Not "match", not "fixture". |
| team game | A game the team plays: a home game or an away game. The only duty of the team at a team game is drive, at an away game: the team never has the table or referee places of its own game (a club rule). | "Team game". Not "own game". |
| home game / away game | A team game in the club's own hall / at another club. | Only for team games: a home game of another club team is a duty game, or just a game. |
| duty game | A home game of another club team where the team has table or referee places. It does not depend on a team game: the team may play that weekend or not. | "Duty game". Not "possible duty game" when the team has places. |
| game list | The list of all games of the club, from the federation's data, that the tool reads. | Not "feed" in user-facing text. |
| event | A training or a game. A duty is not an event; it belongs to a game. | "The next events". Not "activity", not "item". |
| training | A training session of the team on a date. It exists, and counts, only when the coordinator opens a training day or adds a training. | |
| weekly training | A day of the week with a start and an end time, from a date until a date, on which the team trains. It suggests a training day on each of its days. | Not "schedule" (that is for the games), not "training day". |
| training day | A date on which a weekly training suggests a training. It becomes a training only when the coordinator opens it. | Only for a date, never for a day of the week. |
| attendance | Whether a player was at a training or a game, and if not, the reason. Before a game: whether the player comes, does not come, or has not answered. | "Coming", "not coming", "no answer". |
| checked | An event that the coordinator marked as done in the administration: a training, a team game (its attendance and its drive) or a duty game (its places), a game from its start time. It no longer asks for attention: it is not an open item and is not shown as something to do. It can still be opened and corrected. Until then, an event before today is an open item. | "Checked". Not "done" (that is for duties), not "closed". |
| extra player | Someone who plays in one game of the team but is not on its list: a player of another team, or a player who only trains. The tool keeps only their number at the game, not who they are. | "2 extra players". Not "guest". |
| not played | A game that did not, or will not, take place. Its duties and attendance stay stored but do not count. | "Not played". Not "cancelled" (that is for a training). |
| reason | Why a player was not at a training or a game. A reason is valid (for example school) or not valid. | Not "excuse". |
| duty | A task a player must do for a game: drive, table or referee. | Not "task" for these. |
| drive | The duty to drive players to an away game. | |
| table / referee | The duty to fill a table or referee place at a home game of the club, for the team at a duty game. A game has two table places and two referee places. | "Table", not "jury". "Table 1" and "table 2": the tool does not name the tasks at the table. |
| duty place | One place at a game that must be filled. At each home game of the club: referee 1, referee 2, table 1 and table 2, each given to a club team by the referee committee, or still empty. At an away game of the team: one driver. Players of the team fill only the places of the team. | "The 2 table places", "3 drivers". Not "slot", not "spot". |
| duty total | How many duties a player has done. Shows that the work is shared fairly. | |
| message | Text the tool produces for the coordinator to copy into the chat. | The tool never "sends". |
| message of the week | The message for the team and the parents about this week, Monday to Sunday: the games of the team, the drivers, and the table and referee places of the team. | Not "message for the next game" (the older name), not "weekend message". |
| committee message | The message for the referee committee: which player does which table or referee place at the games of this week. | |
| duty overview | A message with all coming duties of one group: driving, or table and referee. It shows who does what, not how much. | "Duty overview". Not "schedule", not "report". |
| report | A table for the coordinator about what each player did, with the details of each player, for example trainings, games and duties. | Not "overview", not "message". |
| contribution | What a player gives to the team: the effort at training and games (attendance, with the reason for each absence), and the duties done. The tool exists to show it. | "Contribution". Not "score", not "points": the tool does not rank players. |
| open item | Something that still needs the coordinator, shown on the start page, for example a past training that is not checked. The tool works it out; the coordinator never adds one. | "3 open items". Not "reminder" (the tool never sends one), not "to do". |
| Season | The page with all events of the season that the tool knows. | "Season" for the page. Not "overview" (that is for a duty overview). |
| referee committee | The club group that gives the table and referee places of the club's home games to the club teams, and wants to know which players do them. | |
